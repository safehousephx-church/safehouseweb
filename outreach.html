Faith That Reaches Beyond Our Walls

Compassion in Action

40+
People experiencing homelessness fed at our last outreach

Before a recent Sunday service, Safe House Church had the privilege of feeding more than 40 people experiencing homelessness in the greater Phoenix area—with hygiene kits, supplies, and meals prepared and packed by our own Serve Team.

This is not a one-time event. This is the DNA of Safe House Church. We serve because every person matters and every act of kindness is a chance to show God's love—not for recognition, because love requires action.
