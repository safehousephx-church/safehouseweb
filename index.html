A Letter From

Pastor Hughes

There are moments in life when God does not give you all the details—He gives you a word. And that word requires faith.

Several years ago, God spoke clearly to my wife, Kyra, and me. He instructed us to leave everything familiar and move to Phoenix, Arizona.

We had never visited Arizona. We had no family there. No friends waiting for us. No financial safety net prepared for the journey.

It did not make sense on paper. It was not convenient. It was not comfortable.

But it was clear.

When God speaks clearly, faith becomes the only appropriate response.

So we packed up our lives and moved. Not because we had all the answers, but because we trusted the One who called us.

Obedience unlocks provision.

God did not reveal every step at once. He opened doors one step at a time. He proved Himself faithful again and again. We learned that faith is not the absence of fear—it is refusing to let fear make the decision.

That journey of faith became the foundation of Safe House Church.

Safe House is more than a church. It is a place where people encounter God's presence, discover their purpose, build strong faith, and find genuine family.

Here, people find faith, family, purpose, and a place to belong. We're committed to helping people become everything God created them to be.

Welcome Home.

Pastor Matthew Hughes

Senior Pastor
Leadership

Pastor Matthew
& Kyra Hughes

Born and raised in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, Pastor Matthew Hughes accepted Christ at an early age and sensed God's call to ministry at just six years old. By thirteen, he preached his first sermon—and has spent his life pursuing that call ever since.

After earning a degree in Biblical Studies and developing a respected career in corporate finance, Pastor Hughes brings a rare ability to bridge faith, leadership, and real-world impact. He ministers throughout the Body of Christ as a conference speaker, revivalist, mentor, and author.

Today, Pastor Hughes and his wife Kyra serve as the founding pastors of Safe House Church in Phoenix, Arizona. His passion: helping people move beyond where they are and become everything God created them to be.
